# The school board pack

Everything a school board's privacy officer, IT department and principals need to decide whether to use
Protégé, and how to run it safely. Written for Ontario first (MFIPPA, the Education Act, EDSTA and its 2026
regulations, AODA), and organized so it carries over to Québec, the rest of Canada, the EU, the UK and the US.

**In one paragraph.** Protégé runs entirely on a computer the school or board controls. That includes its AI
and voice typing. The people who make it receive nothing. Students use nicknames and a picture key, never
real names or email. What they write stays on that computer, is seen only by them and their teacher, and is
deleted by the board's retention rule. Teachers approve every lesson, can read every conversation, and can
correct anything the AI judged. Students are told plainly that Gloam is an AI. It's free and open source
(AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check every claim in these documents against the code.

*These documents explain how the software works. They are not legal advice. The board's privacy officer and
counsel decide how they apply.*
