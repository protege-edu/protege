PROTÉGÉ: students teach, Gloam learns
=====================================

Protégé runs on this laptop, with its own AI. Students join from their Chromebooks with a class code.
Nothing about your students leaves this laptop.

(Most teachers use the installer from https://protege-edu.pages.dev instead of this folder. This folder is
the same Protégé without an installer, for school IT.)

THE FIRST TIME (about 15 minutes, mostly downloading)
1. Double-click "Start Protégé".
2. Your browser opens, and Gloam wakes up: Protégé installs Ollama (the free program that runs Gloam's AI)
   and downloads Gloam's brain (a few GB, once). Make your teacher account while it downloads, and save
   your recovery code.
3. Make a class, add a lesson (or use the built-in one), and press "Show the code".

EVERY CLASS
- Double-click "Start Protégé". Keep the window open and the laptop plugged in and awake.
- Students go to the address it shows (or scan the QR code) and type the class code.
- Students on a different network, or at home? Use "Start Protégé online" instead
  (it needs Tailscale or cloudflared, both free: see the guide on the Protégé website).
- To stop Protégé, close its window.

VOICES (optional)
- The first run asks. Or double-click "Set up voices" (downloads about 850 MB). Then:
  - students can talk to Gloam instead of typing. Their voice is turned into words on this laptop and
    never saved.
  - read-aloud uses Gloam's natural voice, made on this laptop, instead of each device's robotic one.

YOUR DATA
- Everything is saved in your user folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Protege on Windows, ~/.local/share/Protege on Mac.
- A backup is made every day (the last 7 are kept). Updating Protégé never touches your data.

UPDATING
- On the teacher page, "Ready for class?" -> "Check for updates". Download the new version,
  unzip it, and use it instead of this folder. Your classes and students stay.
