# Privacy at a glance

For students, families and teachers. The full details are in the [data inventory](board/data-inventory.html).

## The short version

- **Protégé runs on a computer your school controls.** That includes its AI and voice typing. The people who
  make Protégé never receive anything about anyone.
- **Students use a nickname**, not their name, and sign in with secret pictures. No emails, birthdays, photos
  or addresses are collected.
- **What a student writes to Gloam** is seen by them and their teacher. It isn't sold, shared, used for ads or
  used to train any AI.
- **Gloam is an AI**, and students are told so, in words for their age. Its candles are an estimate of how well
  an idea was explained, never a mark. Teachers can correct them.
- **Nothing tracks anyone.** There are no analytics, ads, trackers or third-party scripts, and the site
  enforces that.
- **Information is deleted** by the school board's rule (by default, a year after a class was last used).
  Backups expire within a week.
- **You can ask** to see, correct or delete a student's information. Ask the teacher, who can download all of
  it in one file.

## Voice typing

When a student presses the mic, a short recording goes to the school's computer, is turned into words there,
and is thrown away. It's never saved or sent to a speech company. The words appear in the student's box so they
can fix them before sending.

## Read-aloud

Gloam's voice is made on the school's computer and played on the student's device. What it reads is never
sent to a speech company or saved.

## Accessibility settings

Students can choose settings like bigger text, read-aloud or a dyslexia-friendly font. These can say something
personal, so a teacher never sees what a student chose for themselves. A teacher only sees the
accommodations they switched on.

## Google

Only if a teacher turns it on for a class, students can sign in with their school Google account. Protégé
then keeps only a scrambled account number: no name, email or photo.

## This website

This website is information, downloads, and the page where students type their class code (/j). Protégé
itself never runs here: lessons, students and what they teach Gloam stay on the school's computer. It has no
cookies, sign-ins, analytics, ads or trackers, and loads nothing from other websites. It remembers one thing,
on your own device only: whether you switched Gloam's voice on.

When a teacher starts **Protégé online**, their computer tells this website where its class is today (a
"room": a short code and the tunnel's address). That's all a room holds: no names, nicknames or anything
students type. The join page looks the code up and sends the student to the class. A room is forgotten after
90 days unused, and a class code someone types is never kept.

It's hosted for free by Cloudflare Pages. Like any website host, Cloudflare sees the internet address of
whoever visits, so it can deliver the pages and block attacks.
